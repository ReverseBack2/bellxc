# XC Scoring 101

Source: Cross Country Meet Scoring, September 2026.

- Every runner earns an individual placement based on speed.
- Teams need at least five runners to earn a team score.
- Runners on teams with fewer than five still receive individual places but are skipped in team scoring.
- Each team’s first seven runners receive placement scores; the first five places are added for the team total.
- Runners six and seven act as blockers by pushing competitors behind them to higher team places.
- Lowest team score wins. If teams tie, the sixth runner’s score breaks the tie.
- Bellarmine example: 2 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 = 24.

Examples from the guide:
- Jonathan Ho finished 4th individually, but his team had fewer than five runners, so he had no team score.
- Gavin Borer was Bellarmine’s sixth finisher and took 10th in team scoring. His score did not enter the five-runner total, but it raised the team place of runners behind him.
